# Sources

Every source this skill and `layout.js` rest on, with its link and its licence or status.

**What was copied: nothing.** Every text and code sample here is written in our own words. Where a licence forbids adaptation, the book is paid, or no licence could be found, only the principle is cited.

**How licences were checked, on 2026-11-24.** Software licences were read from each repository through the GitHub API. Other licences were read on the source's own page; for papers, the licence metadata came from `api.crossref.org`. Where a page showed none, the entry says so.

## Standards
- **W3C** · WCAG 2.2, SC 1.3.2, 1.4.10 and 2.4.11 · https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ · Licence: W3C Document License (2023). Criteria are cited by number and stated in our own words.

## Layout practice
- **Krehel layout** · Jakub Krehel, `better-layout` skill · https://github.com/jakubkrehel/skills · Licence: MIT. Grouping by space with twice the gap between groups, shared edges, controls distinct from content, one primary action, peeking rows, content that bleeds while controls stay in the margins, breakpoints from the content, container queries, room for growth; principles cited.
- **Krehel UI** · Jakub Krehel, `better-ui` skill, `surfaces.md` · https://github.com/jakubkrehel/skills · Licence: MIT. Concentric radius and its limit, optical alignment; principles cited.
- **Salaja** · Raphael Salaja, `userinterface-wiki`, the rules `ux-proximity-grouping`, `ux-similarity-consistency`, `ux-common-region-boundaries`, `ux-uniform-connectedness`, `ux-pragnanz-simplify`, `ux-von-restorff-emphasis`, `visual-concentric-radius`, `visual-consistent-spacing-scale` · https://github.com/raphaelsalaja/userinterface-wiki · Licence: MIT. Principles cited.
- **Vercel** · Vercel, Web Interface Guidelines, the Layout section · https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines · Licence: MIT. Deliberate alignment, optical adjustments, checking wide and narrow screens; principles cited.
- **Kowalski** · Emil Kowalski, `apple-design` skill · https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills · Licence: MIT. Spacing that scales with text, every spacing and alignment value defensible; principles cited.
- **Every Layout** · Heydon Pickering & Andy Bell, Every Layout · https://every-layout.dev/ · Licence: proprietary and paid; its terms forbid re-publishing (terms page, updated 10 July 2019). Only the free pages were read (the rudiments, the axioms, Stack, Sidebar, Switcher). Intrinsic layout without device breakpoints, a scale from one ratio, a sidebar that wraps under a threshold; principles cited, no code taken.
- **Utopia space** · James Gilyead & Trys Mudford, the Utopia space calculator, and Trys Mudford, "Painting with a fluid space palette" (2021) · https://utopia.fyi/space/calculator/ · Licence: none shown on the site. Fluid space steps and pairs from one base; the method is cited.
- **Refactoring UI** · Adam Wathan & Steve Schoger, Refactoring UI · https://www.refactoringui.com/ · Licence: paid book, © The UI Company. Only its public table of contents was read: de-emphasise to emphasise, labels as a last resort, start with too much white space, a spacing system, fewer borders; principles cited.
- **Self-Made Web Designer** · the YouTube channel Self-Made Web Designer, three videos as summarised by the user: "Master Website Layouts in Under 10 Minutes", "Ultimate Website Layout Guide: 17 Swipeable Examples", "33 Unique Website Layouts with Real World Project Examples" · https://selfmadewebdesigner.com/ · Licence: none read. Hierarchy, intentional variation and balance; a column grid as a guide; one to six content columns by purpose; swiping rows on a phone; testing the fullest and emptiest content. Principles cited from the user's notes.
- **shadcn** · shadcn/ui, `--radius` and the radii derived from it · https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui · Licence: MIT. Token names used.
- **Tailwind** · Tailwind CSS theme, `--spacing`, `--container-*`, `--breakpoint-*`, `--radius-*` · https://github.com/tailwindlabs/tailwindcss · Licence: MIT. Token names used.
- **Studio** · this project's own method, its interview with the user and its measure of 15 home pages · https://github.com/MartinMrnn/design-engineer-toolkit · Licence: MIT.

## Research
- **Laws of UX** · Jon Yablonski, Laws of UX · https://lawsofux.com/ · Licence: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. The names of the six visual laws `ux` hands to this skill; no text adapted.
- **Wertheimer 1923** · M. Wertheimer, "Untersuchungen zur Lehre von der Gestalt II", *Psychologische Forschung* 4 · https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00410640 · Licence: publisher's text-and-data-mining terms only, no open licence. Grouping by proximity and similarity, and the tendency to the simplest form; principles cited.
- **von Restorff 1933** · H. von Restorff, "Über die Wirkung von Bereichsbildungen im Spurenfeld", *Psychologische Forschung* 18 · https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02409636 · Licence: publisher's text-and-data-mining terms only, no open licence. The isolated item is remembered best; principle cited.
- **Palmer 1992** · S. E. Palmer, "Common region: A new principle of perceptual grouping", *Cognitive Psychology* · https://doi.org/10.1016/0010-0285(92)90014-S · Licence: publisher's text-and-data-mining terms only, no open licence. Principle cited.
- **Palmer & Rock 1994** · S. E. Palmer & I. Rock, "Rethinking perceptual organization: The role of uniform connectedness", *Psychonomic Bulletin & Review* · https://doi.org/10.3758/BF03200760 · Licence: publisher's text-and-data-mining terms only, no open licence. Principle cited.
- **Wagemans 2012** · J. Wagemans et al., "A century of Gestalt psychology in visual perception: I. Perceptual grouping and figure-ground organization", *Psychological Bulletin* · https://doi.org/10.1037/a0029333 · Licence: none in its metadata. The review that frames the laws above as findings with limits; principle cited.
