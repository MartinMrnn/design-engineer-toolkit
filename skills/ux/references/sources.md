# Sources

Every source this skill rests on, with its link and its licence or status.

**What was copied: nothing.** Every text here is written in our own words. Sources under MIT are credited as references even though no passage of theirs was adapted, so no third-party text is redistributed. Where a licence forbids adaptation or none could be found, only the principle is cited.

## Standards
- **W3C** · WCAG 2.2, W3C Recommendation of 12 December 2024 · https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ · Licence: W3C Document License (2023). Criteria are cited by number and stated in our own words.

Principles only.

It is credited as the reference that gathers these laws; each law is named, attributed to its original author and described in our own words.

## Design engineers
- **Salaja** · Raphael Salaja, userinterface.wiki and its skill · https://www.userinterface.wiki/laws-of-ux and https://github.com/raphaelsalaja/userinterface-wiki · Licence: MIT. Informed the way laws become practical rules.
- **Vercel** · Web Interface Guidelines · https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines · Licence: MIT. Informed forms, navigation state, copy and destructive actions.
- **Kowalski** · Emil Kowalski, `break-ui` skill · https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills · Licence: MIT. Informed testing with worst-case data.
- **Krehel** · Jakub Krehel, `make-interfaces-feel-better` · https://github.com/jakubkrehel/make-interfaces-feel-better · Licence: MIT. Informed the touch target recommendation.
- **UXMD** · Gavin Nelson, UXMD · https://github.com/gavinmn/uxmd · Licence: MIT. Informed the idea that each new set of actions is a state.
- **Nelson** · Gavin Nelson, "GitHub Navigation Shortcuts" and "Linear Navigation" · https://nelson.co/work/navigation-shortcuts and https://nelson.co/work/linear-26 · Licence: none found, principles only. Informed teaching in context, recent items and navigation within reach.
- **Freiberg** · Rauno Freiberg, Web Interface Guidelines · https://interfaces.rauno.me/ · Licence: none found, principles only. Informed forgiving interaction, optimistic updates, forms and empty states.
- **Coursey** · Paco Coursey, "Redesign 2021" · https://paco.me/writing/redesign-2021 · Licence: none found, principles only. Informed keeping content readable when a script fails.
