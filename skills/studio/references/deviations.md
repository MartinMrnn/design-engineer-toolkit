# Deviations from the approved design

Every difference a visitor could see between the approved mockups or motion and `site/` that the frontend developer could not avoid, one row each, written at D1 or E1. The critic judges each justification and its impact. A deviation is not accepted because it is listed: the user approves or refuses each one, and `design/decisions.md` records the approved ones. A refused deviation is fixed and its row removed.
