# The nine steps

Read a step's section before starting it. Copy its "Done when" into the mission as is: the agent meets the criteria about its own work, and you check those about the critic, the publication and the user before closing the step. Charter paths below are under `design/charter/project/`.

## A1 · Kickoff (you)
- **Inputs**: what the user knows about the client, the existing site, any creative material.
- **Interview**: 2 or 3 rounds of at most 3 questions, by theme: goal of the site, priority visitor, main action, available content (copy, photos, logo), scope of the mockup (which pages), deadline, new site or redesign, tone, language of the site's copy. The language of our conversation is not a question: it is the language of the user's first message. Skip what the user's material already answers. Ask the user's name if you do not know it (it signs the charter's changes).
- **Colour**: Colour is one more theme inside these rounds, not an extra round (still at most 3 rounds of at most 3 questions). The colour theme is three questions: what the site should make people feel, in three words; whether the project already has a visual identity, a logo or colours already in use, and how binding they are (keep / may evolve / free), with the colours to avoid, and why, in the same question; how present colour should be, the appetite (discreet / present / dominant), and whether it is a preference or a constraint. Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/colour/references/intent.md`, only that file, and write the `Colour intent` line of `01-context.md` as it shows, appetite included. "No idea yet" is a fine answer to any of these; never invent an identity.
- **Material**: file everything the user gives. Images and counter-examples go in `design/inspiration/`; links, with one line on what they like about each, in `design/inspiration/inspiration.md`; colour or font ideas in `design/inspiration/notes.md`. A logo goes in `assets/Logos/` of the charter as well.
- **Outputs**: the charter (`charter.py init`, then `README.md` and `01-context.md`) and its Artifact; `CLAUDE.md`; `.claude/launch.json`; `design/decisions.md`, `design/feedback.md`, `design/deviations.md`; `design/reviews/scripts/capture.js` and `checks.js`.
- **Order**: write `CLAUDE.md` first, with the user's name; then the charter's `README.md` and `01-context.md`; get the brief approved and record it in `design/decisions.md`; then create and publish the charter.
- **Supporting pages**: not a question. `01-context.md` always plans a 404 page and a legal notice, plus terms of sale when the site sells and a privacy policy when a form collects personal data. Ask only if the user's material leaves a doubt (does the site sell?).
- **Done when**: `01-context.md` fits on one page and states the goal, priority visitor, main action, scope, supporting pages, available content, deadline, tone and colour intent ("no idea yet" counts); every unknown fact is `[to provide: …]`; `charter.py check --step A1` passes; the charter is published and its URL is in `CLAUDE.md`; every other output exists; the user approves the brief.

## B1 · Product (ux)
- **Inputs**: `README.md`, `01-context.md`.
- **Outputs**: `design/ux/research.md`, `design/ux/captures/`; then `02-intent.md`, `03-story.md`, `04-positioning.md`, `05-voice-and-wording.md`.
- **Order**: research first; its REPORT asks the user to pick the key messages; resumed with the choice, the UX designer writes the four product sections.
- **Done when**: every site studied has at least one capture and its findings; the research states what visitors expect and offers 2 or 3 key messages, each linked to a finding; the user picks the key messages; the four sections are written in the site's language, `04-positioning.md` links each chosen message to its finding and `05-voice-and-wording.md` holds five real examples; `charter.py check --step B1` passes; the user approves the product sections.

## C1 · Moodboard reading and ideas (art-director, critic)
- **When**: C1 starts right after A1, alongside B1: the reading needs only the brief. Launch the UX designer (B1) and the art director (C1) in one message. Each captures in its own browser context. The idea cards wait for B1: once the user approves the product sections, resume the C1 art director with them.
- **Before**: if `design/inspiration/` holds no references, ask the user for them now: images, sites with one line on what they like, counter-examples.
- **Inputs**: `design/inspiration/`, `01-context.md` (its `Colour intent` line included), the taste file; for the idea cards, `02-intent.md` to `05-voice-and-wording.md`.
- **Outputs**: `07-visual-intent.md` (Keep, Avoid, Keywords, Colours seen, then Ideas), `design/inspiration/captures/`, four idea cards `design/ideas/a.md` to `d.md`; the critic's review of the cards, `design/reviews/C1-ideas-<round>.md`; in the C1 entry of `design/decisions.md`, a line `Ideas: a <name>, b <name>, c <name>, d <name>`, in the art director's order of recommendation.
- **Critic**: the distinctness and relevance of the four cards, on text (the critique skill, "C1 · the idea cards"), with a verdict per card: keep, rework or replace. Before any specimen is built, every card marked rework or replace goes back to the art director once (round 2, `missions.md`): it reworks the card, replaces it with another idea, or drops it when no relevant idea is left (the specimens then go on with three); the critic then reviews the changed cards (round 2).
- **The user**: approves the reading. Show the cards only when the critic still marks one rework or replace after round 2 (the user then decides), or when the user asks to see them.
- **Done when**: every reference is analysed, with a capture, and read as relations; contradictions between references are settled with the user; Colours seen holds the colours read with `colour.py dominant` on the captures (as colours seen in the references, not tokens) and their character against the Colour intent; the four idea cards exist (three when one was dropped in round 2), each with its idea, manifestations, what it refuses, why it fits, the accessibility at risk and its source; the critic keeps each card, or the user has settled it; the C1 entry of `design/decisions.md` holds the `Ideas:` line; `charter.py check --step C1` passes; the user approves the reading.

## B2 · Structure and content (ux, critic)
- **Inputs**: the charter up to `05-voice-and-wording.md`, the research.
- **Outputs**: `06-structure.md`, the priority schema with the real copy; a `Wireframe<Name>` card (and its `…Mobile` copy, `charter.py cards`) only where a functional need calls for one.
- **What is fixed, what stays open**: B2 fixes the intents, the main path, the needs, the rank of each element and the copy. The layout, how each rank is expressed and how each content is shown stay open to the art direction, which may propose a change of structure: the user approves it, and the UX designer writes it (mode `amend`, `missions.md`).
- **Critic**: relevance, usability, on `06-structure.md` (text) and any wireframe card.
- **Done when**: the sitemap matches the scope exactly, supporting pages included; each page states the visitor intents it serves, and every section and feature traces to one of them; each section ranks its elements and states its needs; the main path is written step by step; the mobile plan is written as constraints (what the first screen at 390 px must show, the navigation pattern and why, where the main action stays reachable); every section holds its real copy, following `05-voice-and-wording.md`, with `[to provide: …]` for missing facts; any wireframe card names the functional need it serves; `charter.py check --step B2` passes; the critic accepts it; the user approves the structure and the copy.

## C2 · Specimens, then directions (art-director, critic)
C2 has two rounds: four light specimens, one per idea card, judged in three real situations; then the one or two the user keeps, deepened into directions.

### Round 1 · Specimens (one art director)
- **Launch**: one art-director agent, `produce` mode, with the four idea cards of the `Ideas:` line. No interview: it starts from C1 and B2. It builds specimens `a` and `b`, reports, and you resume the same agent for `c` and `d` (its turn limit counts per run; the resume keeps its context, `missions.md`).
- **Inputs**: the four idea cards and the critic's C1 review of them (`design/reviews/C1-ideas-<round>.md`: what the cards repeat, the accessibility they miss), `06-structure.md` (ranks and copy), `07-visual-intent.md`, `design/inspiration/` and its captures, the taste file.
- **Outputs**: per specimen, `design/specimens/<id>/`: `index.html`, the three situations from `06-structure.md` with the real copy (the opening, one dense section, one interaction with its states), at 1440 px and 390 px; `tokens.json`, a partial palette and type, and `tokens.css`; `idea.md`, the card completed with the distribution argued, the accessibility conflicts met and the alternatives tried, and any structural proposal; and the views of `design/reviews/scripts/views.js` (normal, grey, blur, at both widths, in `design/reviews/captures/`). No Artifact, no documented component, no Cover. The idea's text never appears in `index.html`.
- **Colour**: `colour.py palette` on each specimen's `tokens.json` while it is built.
- **Critic**, in two phases: blind, with the four preview links only; then, resumed with the four cards, the idea and the execution of each specimen, distinctiveness, coherence, craft, whether the four are distinct ideas, any structural proposal's relevance, and the accessibility block. Item `specimens`.
- **Present**: the board (`references/board.html`, one option per specimen, its idea's name and sentence as the pitch, the critic's verdict), with the four links. The user keeps two, or one when it clearly leads, or combines two ideas (that makes a new specimen, built by the same art director, then reviewed). Approved structural proposals go to the UX designer (`missions.md`, "Structural proposal").
- **Done when**: four specimens exist with their situations, palette run, idea completed and views; the critic has written its blind reading before its phase 2; the board is shown; the user keeps one or two (or says "choose for me" and the independent comparison is applied); the C2 entry of `design/decisions.md` holds `Specimens kept: <ids>`.

### Round 2 · Directions (one art director per kept specimen)
- **Launch**: you run `charter.py branch <ids>` with the kept specimens' ids, then launch one art-director agent per branch in one message, each with its specimen and its card. No interview.
- **Ideas**: the directions differ by their ideas. Colour takes the distribution each idea calls for; two directions may share a colour strategy. The appetite of the `Colour intent` line is a preference, or a constraint when marked so; it assigns no strategy.
- **Inputs**: the branch (a copy of the charter, `07-visual-intent.md` included), the specimen and its `idea.md`, `design/inspiration/` and its captures, the taste file.
- **Outputs**: per branch, a sketch of the direction in `design/branches/<id>/project/` (every token, its palette built with the colour method; `08-colour.md` to `13-rules.md` short, `08-colour.md` with its part Why these colours, which starts with the `Strategy:` line when a strategy names the distribution (the label `Strategy:` and the strategy names stay in English, the board and the critic read them, like the label `Colour intent` in `01-context.md`); `14-sources.md` complete; the rules of `README.md` short; `Button` and `Card` in their normal and hover states, `Link`, `Field` and `Nav` left as starters; `Cover`; `Showcase` complete: the three situations of the specimen, built on the branch's tokens and components, composed for the idea), its Artifact, and `design/branches/<id>/captures/` (`cover.png`, `showcase-1440.png` and `showcase-390.png`, the whole Showcase, `components.png`, which frames Button and Card, and the views of `views.js` on the Showcase). Diagnostic variants are made by hand, only to answer a question (`diagnosis.md`). Then you build and publish `design/board/`.
- **Colour**: each art director runs `colour.py palette` on the branch before it is published, and `colour.py dominant --match` on `showcase-1440.png` to see the visible hierarchy (the paths and the mission's `Colour skill:` are in the art-direction skill).
- **Critic**, in two phases: first blind, with the preview links only (its blind reading is written before it sees any card); then, resumed with the cards, the idea and the execution of each direction, distinctiveness, coherence, craft, whether the directions are clearly distinct, and the accessibility block; colour is judged against the colour intent, the context, the coherence of the direction and the hierarchy, and by whether the composition puts the colour to work as its idea says. Item `directions`.
- **Mix**: no family is taken from another direction by default. A `--take` stays possible when the user asks; then the base instance recomposes the Showcase's three situations and the critic reviews the coherence of the result before the charter is published.
- **Done when**: each branch passes `charter.py check --step C2 --sketch --root design/branches/<id>`, `colour.py palette` has been run on it, its `08-colour.md` holds Why these colours, and it is published; the directions are clearly distinct ideas; none repeats a past direction of the taste file; the critic has written its blind reading before its phase 2, and accepts each direction the user keeps (a rejected one is presented as rejected); after round 2 the art director republishes its branch, and the lead runs `board` again and republishes it; the board is published; the user chooses (or says "choose for me" and the independent comparison's recommendation is applied); `charter.py merge` is run; the base instance has completed the charter (`Link`, `Field`, `Nav`, the missing states of `Button` and `Card`, sections 08 to 13 and the `README.md` rules in full, `13-rules.md` and `14-sources.md` revised); `charter.py check --step C2` (the full check) passes on the charter; the charter is published; the branches are renamed.

## C3 · Key mockups (art-director, critic)
- **Inputs**: the charter (tokens, components, `06-structure.md` and any wireframe card), decisions.
- **Outputs**: the home options `design/mockups/home-a.html`, `home-b.html`… and a board; after the choice, `components/MockupHome/preview.html`, then `components/Mockup<Page>/preview.html` for every other page in scope and `components/MockupNotFound/preview.html`; every new component in the charter; `14-sources.md` updated.
- **Critic**: all five criteria, and the base components completed at C2 (states, focus visibility, contrast in every state).
- **Done when**: every page in scope, and the 404 page, has a mockup card that works at 1440 px and 390 px using only the charter's tokens and components; the opening hero fills the first screen at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844 without showing the next section, and loses nothing on shorter screens; the mobile navigation follows the B2 plan; the chosen direction's distribution is applied consistently across the pages; `charter.py check --step C3` passes; the critic accepts; the user picks one layout per page.

## C4 · Motion (art-director, critic)
- **Inputs**: the `MockupHome` card, the charter, `07-visual-intent.md`.
- **Outputs**: `design/motion/a.html`, `b.html` (and `c.html`) and a board; after the choice, the `duration` and `easing` tokens, the `Motion` card, `12-motion.md` completed.
- **Critic**: consistency, craft, usability.
- **Done when**: every animation states its role, duration and easing; the reduced-motion version keeps all content visible; the critic accepts; the user picks one personality; the chosen motion is in the charter as tokens, the `Motion` card and `12-motion.md`; `charter.py freeze` and `charter.py check --step C4` pass.

## D1 · Mockup build (frontend, critic)
- **Interview**: what must be interactive and what can stay visual.
- **Inputs**: the charter (tokens, components, `Mockup…` and `Motion` cards, `12-motion.md`, `13-rules.md`, `14-sources.md`), decisions.
- **Outputs**: `site/` (its `assets/css/tokens.css`, `components.css`, fonts and images from `charter.py export site/assets`); a row in `design/deviations.md` for each deviation that cannot be avoided.
- **Order**: the home page first, then the critic, then the user; then each other page type the same way. Each mission covers one page type.
- **Critic**: consistency (fidelity to the mockup card, and the justification of each deviation), craft, usability, per page type.
- **Supporting pages**: built with the last page type: `site/404.html` from its mockup, and `site/legal.html`, one simple page in the same layout whose sections (legal notice, and terms or privacy when `01-context.md` plans them) hold `[to provide: …]`. The footer links point to its anchors.
- **Done when** (for the page type of the mission): its pages exist in `site/` and open through the preview server; only the charter's tokens and components are used; the agreed interactions work; the developer ran one comparison pass per page at 1440 px and 390 px; every visible difference is fixed or recorded in `design/deviations.md` with its justification; the critic accepts the page type; the user approves it and each of its deviations. D1 ends when every page in scope has been through this.

## E1 · Final review (critic, then frontend)
- **Critic**: all five criteria, plus `checks.js` on every page.
- **Done when**: no console errors; no horizontal overflow at 390 px; no broken images or empty links; no serious accessibility violation; reduced motion keeps every page readable; every criterion at 3 or above; every row of `design/deviations.md` is approved by the user; `charter.py check --step E1` passes and the charter is published; the user approves the final mockup.
- **Closing**: propose an update of the taste file (what the user liked and rejected, the direction used and the charter's link) and write it only with their consent; ask the three closing questions of `design/feedback.md`; tell the user that `site/` is ready to send, as a folder or on any static host, and that the charter stays on claude.ai as the project's reference.
