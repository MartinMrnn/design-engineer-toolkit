# Sources

Every source this skill and `imagery.js` rest on, with its link and its licence or status.

**What was copied: nothing.** Every text and code sample here is written in our own words. Where a licence forbids adaptation, or no licence could be found, only the principle is cited. Each idea is credited to the author who wrote it: the 2 % noise against banding is Nelson's, "never glass on glass" is Kowalski's.

**How licences were checked, on 2026-11-27.** Software licences were read from each repository through the GitHub API. Other licences were read on the source's own page. The Unsplash and Pexels pages refused our automated browser; their terms were read through a web search of their own pages and help centres, and are summarised, not quoted.

## Practice
- **Freiberg depth** · Rauno Freiberg, "Designing Depth" (July 2024) · https://rauno.me/craft/depth · Licence: none shown. Composition by layers, a foreground that "dirties the frame", blur and fade to recede, ambient objects made of the main subjects, a dimmed or blurred backdrop under an overlay; principles cited. Its parts on choreography and staggering belong to `motion`.
- **Krehel UI** · Jakub Krehel, `better-ui` skill (`SKILL.md`, `surfaces.md`, `icons.md`) · https://github.com/jakubkrehel/skills · Licence: MIT. Shadows for elevation and borders for structure, a ring of layered shadows with a transparent border for forced colours, a white ring on dark grounds, the 1 px image outline in pure black or white, icon stroke by text weight, one library, `currentColor`; principles cited.
- **Krehel shadows** · Jakub Krehel, "Using shadows instead of borders" · https://jakub.kr/work/shadows · Licence: none shown. Three transparent layers instead of a border adapt to images and coloured grounds; principle cited.
- **Nelson renders** · Gavin Nelson, "Linear homepage renders" · https://nelson.co/work/linear-renders · Licence: none shown. Iterating by taste on a minimal scene; volumetric light; reflections that make text unreadable; lowering opacity rather than lightness to push back; 2 % monochrome noise at 50 % grey in overlay against banding; flattening renders to match the page; removing depth of field; principles cited.
- **Comeau shadows** · Josh W. Comeau, "Designing Beautiful Shadows in CSS" · https://www.joshwcomeau.com/css/designing-shadows/ · Licence: © Joshua Comeau, all rights reserved. Elevation directs attention; one light for every shadow; higher means larger offset and blur, lower opacity; layering; shadows in the ground's hue; principles cited.
- **Kowalski** · Emil Kowalski, `apple-design` and `emil-design-eng` skills · https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills · Licence: MIT. Translucent materials as a floating layer, never a translucent surface on another, fallbacks for reduced transparency, legible text over translucency, the cost of heavy blur, eased gradient stops; principles cited.
- **Salaja** · Raphael Salaja, `userinterface-wiki`, rules `visual-layered-shadows`, `visual-shadow-direction`, `visual-shadow-matches-elevation`, `visual-animate-shadow-pseudo` · https://github.com/raphaelsalaja/userinterface-wiki · Licence: MIT. Layered shadows, one direction, an elevation scale, animating a pseudo-element's opacity; principles cited. Its rule against pure black shadows is not followed: the measure finds black at low alpha on neutral grounds.
- **Vercel** · Vercel, Web Interface Guidelines, Design and Performance sections · https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines · Licence: MIT. Layered shadows, hue consistency on coloured grounds, gradient banding, explicit image dimensions, preloading only the first screen, video over GIF with a still; principles cited.
- **Tailwind** · Tailwind CSS theme, `--shadow-*`, `--blur-*` · https://github.com/tailwindlabs/tailwindcss · Licence: MIT. Token names used.
- **Doron Studio** · Doron Studio, "Why Most Designers Get Texture Wrong" (YouTube), as summarised by the user · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6R4v60jM1UU · Licence: none read. Micro and macro detail, a texture brought to mid grey before an overlay blend; principles cited from the user's notes.
- **Framer University** · Framer University, "Top 8 Ways to Create Beautiful Website Backgrounds", and a Figma textures tutorial (YouTube), as summarised by the user · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMrR-NqyGSk · Licence: none read. Light built from layered soft shapes, backgrounds of light and texture placed behind content; principles cited from the user's notes.
- **Studio** · this project's own method, its interview with the user and its measures of 35 gallery sites and 20 home pages · https://github.com/MartinMrnn/design-engineer-toolkit · Licence: MIT.
